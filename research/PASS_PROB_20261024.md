## One line
**No sizing trick or new family creates an edge.** Because the eval has **no time limit**, pass odds from 9,954 are
≈ 25–28% for any sane risk size (zero-edge baseline 27%). Bigger risk mostly makes it end sooner.

## Key insights
1. **This is gambler's ruin.** The fair, frictionless reference is 554/(554+1,046) = 34.6%. Three things drop it to ~26%:
   - the "skip if a stop would breach" buffer, which effectively moves the floor to 9,400 + risk + 30;
   - cost drag of ~0.09R per trade (0.06R commission + 0.03R funding);
   - payoff skew.
2. **Sizing trades time for nothing.** $100 resolves in ~2 months with similar final odds to $150/$200 at 1 month / 2–3 weeks.
3. **The daily-line cap is ≈ $270/trade.** A trade held past 08:30 Shanghai gets a new line at 0.97 × entry balance,
   so risk above ~$270 can breach on a normal stop.
4. **Most failures are "locked", not breached.** The account survives but can't take the standard trade.

## Overfitting / caveats

- The intra-trade target touch is approximated by MFE.
