## Model A — Monte Carlo (primary)
- 20,000 paths per (distribution, rule). i.i.d. bootstrap of (gap, trade) pairs. Continuous clock in hours;
  daily reset every 24h at 00:30 UTC; the path starts at a random time-of-day.
- Daily floor = max(9,400, 0.97 × balance at reset). Before each entry: allowed iff risk + $30 buffer ≤ equity − daily floor
  AND risk + $30 ≤ equity − 9,400. If not allowed by the daily floor only → wait until next reset (signal skipped).
  If not allowed by the static floor at the rule's minimum risk → **LOCKED** (counted with fail; reported separately).
- Pass if equity + MFE_R × risk ≥ 11,000 during a trade (MFE is assumed reachable before stop for winners; for losers
  MFE occurs before the stop by construction). Breach if equity + MAE_R×risk ≤ active floor (only possible via gaps, R<−1).
- Horizons reported: 30, 60, 90, 180, 365 days, and "ultimate" = 1,095 days (≈ no time limit). Status at horizon:
  PASS / FAIL(breach) / LOCKED / RUNNING. Expected days to resolution (median over resolved paths).

## Model B — historical paths (cross-check)

## Rules (fixed list; no additions after the lock)
- R_FIX_{50,100,150,200,250,300}: fixed $ risk; skip if the stop-out would breach (current standing rule). Min risk = cap.
- R_FLOORFRAC: risk = clamp(0.25 × (equity − 9,400), 50, 300).
- R_SNAP (bold play): risk = clamp((11,000 − equity) / 3.6, 50, 300), i.e. one clean TP ≈ reaches target; still subject to
  the daily/static check, shrinking to available headroom − 30 if ≥ 50.
- R_DAILYSHRINK150: base 150; if the daily headroom is short, shrink to headroom − 30 (if ≥ 50) instead of skipping.
- R_FREQ2_{100,200}: R_FIX with all gaps halved (proxy for adding a 2nd uncorrelated instrument with the SAME per-trade
  distribution; optimistic, since earlier altcoin study found ETH trades negative recently and corr 0.82).

## Pre-registered decision rule
Recommend replacing R_FIX_100 with rule X only if, under BOTH D1 and D3:
  (a) ultimate P(pass) of X ≥ R_FIX_100 + 5 pp, and (b) X is not worse than R_FIX_100 on P(pass) at 60 days by > 2 pp.
D2 may be reported but cannot be the basis of a recommendation. If no rule meets (a)+(b), verdict = "keep $100".
Among rules meeting the test, pick the one with the highest D3 ultimate P(pass); report its added P(fail) and its
expected days. Analytic cross-check: zero-drift gambler's ruin P(pass) = 554.28 / (554.28 + 1,045.72) = 34.6%
(upper reference for a fair, frictionless, continuous game); fee drag per trade and lock-out must push results below it.
